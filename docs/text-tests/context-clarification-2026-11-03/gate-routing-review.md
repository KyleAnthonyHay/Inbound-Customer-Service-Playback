## Context-gate evidence and limitations

The identical ambiguous track opening now elicits neutral questions in both branches. Console: “Where are you turning that track down—what screen or piece of equipment are you using when you hear the click and guide change together?” App: “Where are you turning that track down—what screen or device are you using when you notice the click and guide change together?” Only after LV1 versus Playback is supplied does technical guidance begin.

Regular01 now asks, “Where are you turning that guide level down—on the Playback screen, or on another control you can see?” The caller states it is on the sound desk. The resulting routing investigation and fix therefore follow actual caller-established control context. This addresses the concrete initial gate failure retained in C; it does not prove the question will be generated deterministically across future calls.

Clear-console keeps the already supplied LV1 context. Regular03 shows the countervailing inefficiency: its opening already says the song looks like it is playing **in Playback**, yet the first reply asks whether that is on Playback’s screen or somewhere else. The caller repeats the already-known fact. This redundant clarification is preserved as a limitation rather than a gate success to praise.
